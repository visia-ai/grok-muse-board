## TL;DR

Semrush is too expensive if you only need the basics. On 30 October 2026 the cheapest public plan, SEO, was **$117.33 per month billed annually**, instead of **$139 per month**, for 5 websites and 500 keywords tracked daily.

For a Semrush-shaped suite, start with **SE Ranking Core**: **$103.20** on the annual tab, or **$129.00 per month** without that discount, with 10 projects and 2,000 keywords a day. The saving is real and small. SE Ranking Growth costs more than Semrush’s SEO and Starter plans.

Under $100, use **Serpstat Individual at $50 per month** (annual invoice **[PRICE TO VERIFY]**; the Team price is also **[PRICE TO VERIFY]**, $100 on the plans page and $129 on the trial page) or **Mangools Basic at $61 per month or $452.40 per year**. The lowest paid entry is **LowFruits Standard at $20.75 per month billed yearly**, or **$29.9 per month**. For drafts and AI-answer checks, use **Writesonic Starter at $79 per month billed annually** and keep a rank tracker. Writesonic Basic and Growth cost more than Semrush SEO. **[NEEDS HANDS-ON: trial SE Ranking Core and Semrush SEO on one shared keyword list before choosing.]**

## How to choose

**Near $100–$130, suite shape.** Trial SE Ranking Core. Annual billing is $103.20 against Semrush SEO at $117.33, with a larger published keyword cap. The gap is small. The affiliate note is above if you would rather ignore a tool this site may earn from.

**Well under $100.** Serpstat Individual is the $50 card, with the annual total and the Team conflict still open. Mangools Basic is $61 a month or $452.40 a year. Pick Serpstat when you want audit and backlinks on that Individual card. Pick Mangools when the work is lookups plus about 200 tracked keywords on weekly updates.

**About $30 or less.** LowFruits Standard: 3,000 credits, no rollover, 100 tracked keywords.

**The expensive work is writing.** Writesonic Starter covers 15 articles and a narrow AI check. Keep one of the other four if you still need positions. Basic and Growth do not lower a Semrush bill.

**Many sites, Share of Voice, or long history.** Price higher tiers on the day you buy. Semrush puts Share of Voice on custom Advanced and 15 websites on Pro+ at $248.17 per month billed annually. SE Ranking puts all-time history on Growth. Mangools Agency is $141 a month. Several of those tiers cost more than Semrush’s entry plan.

## FAQ

### Semrush is too expensive. What are the best alternatives?

For a suite, trial SE Ranking Core ($103.20 per month on the annual tab versus Semrush SEO at $117.33). Under $100, use Serpstat Individual at $50 per month or Mangools Basic at $61 per month or $452.40 per year. For the lowest bill, use LowFruits Standard at $20.75 per month billed yearly. For articles and AI mentions, use Writesonic Starter and keep a rank tracker. “Best” means the closest published fit at a lower entry price, not a hands-on score.

### Is SE Ranking cheaper than Semrush?

Core is, on the 30 October 2026 pages: $103.20 versus $117.33 per month on annual billing, and $129 versus $139 on the monthly figures. Growth, at $223.20 on the annual tab, costs more than Semrush SEO and Starter.

### What is the cheapest Semrush alternative for rank tracking?

LowFruits Standard, at $20.75 per month billed yearly or $29.9 per month, with 100 tracked keywords. Mangools Basic tracks 200, on weekly updates in the page copy. SE Ranking Core tracks 2,000 a day, close to Semrush’s price.

### Can Writesonic replace Semrush?

Starter can cover a capped number of drafts and checks on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. It does not list Semrush’s rank and backlink package. Basic and Growth cost more than Semrush SEO.

### Is there a free alternative to Semrush?

Mangools publishes a free account with no credit card and, in its pricing script, 5 lookups per 24 hours. That shows the interface. It does not replace a paid rank tracker. The other products publish trials or refunds with the limits above.

### Do the cheaper plans include AI search tracking?

SE Ranking Core lists 25 prompts a day on 5 engines. Writesonic Starter lists 50 a day on three products. Serpstat lists AI Visibility from Team up, and those two official pages disagree on the Team price. Semrush SEO already lists AI-search tracking. **[NEEDS HANDS-ON: run five real customer questions. A prompt cap is not answer quality.]**
